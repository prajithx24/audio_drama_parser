Modern Shanmugi
EPISODE 1: The Burden of Debt
It was morning in a busy suburban area of Salem.
Sathya, Arjun's younger sister, was busy looking through a catalogue of wedding silk sarees. As she checked the price of each saree, she said, "Anna, this saree looks beautiful, but it's too expensive."
Arjun was sitting beside her, browsing job websites on his old laptop. His face was filled with worry.
"That's okay, Sathya. Just pick whichever one you like. After all, you get married only once."
Sathya smiled. She had no idea how hard Arjun was struggling.
"Anna, I know how much you're going through. But you don't have to do all this for me."
With a smile, Arjun held Sathya's hand.
"Don't talk like that. You're my only sister. I want your wedding to be special."
But his mind was occupied with completely different thoughts. His education loan, the mortgage on their house and the huge amount of money needed for Sathya's wedding were weighing heavily on him.
In one corner of the room, on top of an old wooden cupboard, lay a pile of letters from various banks. Every single one was a reminder demanding repayment of their loans.
Arjun deliberately avoided looking at them.
"Arjun!" his mother's voice called out.
"Go and get some milk. I have to prepare breakfast."
Arjun got up and headed to the milk shop.
As he walked down the street, his mind was filled with worries.
Everything has changed. If Dad were still alive, we wouldn't be in this situation. Now, Mom and my sister are depending entirely on me. What am I going to do? Where am I going to find so much money?
He let out a deep sigh.
The reason behind Arjun's debt was his father's sudden death. After his father passed away, the entire responsibility of the family fell on Arjun's shoulders.
His father had been running a small grocery store. But after his death, the business started declining, and the debts kept piling up.
Arjun was an engineering graduate, but the salaries offered by the jobs he managed to find were nowhere near enough to clear their debts.
"Anna!"
Sathya came running towards him in a hurry.
"The wedding hall owner called. He says we still haven't paid the advance. He told us that if we don't pay by tomorrow, he'll book the hall for someone else."
Arjun froze.
"Oh no! Wait, Sathya. I'm still searching for a job. I'll find something soon."
Sathya looked at him, confused.
"What are you saying, Anna? Will we be able to arrange the money by tomorrow? What are we going to do now?"
Arjun didn't say anything. He clenched his teeth.
The financial crisis was eating him up from inside.
________________________________________
That evening, Arjun went to his friend Durai's house.
Durai was his childhood friend and had always stood by him through every problem. He ran a small e-service centre.
"What's wrong, Arjun? You look completely down. You haven't looked yourself since morning," Durai said as soon as he saw him.
Arjun sank into a chair.
"What should I even say? Looks like we're going to lose the wedding hall. They want the advance by tomorrow."
Durai was shocked.
"Oh, God! How are we supposed to arrange that much money in such a short time?"
Arjun shook his head.
"I don't know, da. I've been searching for jobs continuously. But nothing suitable for my qualification is coming my way. The big companies reject me because I don't have enough experience. The small companies don't offer enough salary. I honestly don't know what to do."
Durai thought for a while.
"Okay, listen. I know someone who owns a fashion company. I'll ask him if there's any vacancy. But... I think it's a department meant only for women."
"Only for women?" Arjun raised his eyebrows. "What kind of job will I get there?"
"I don't know, da. But it's a big company, and there's a chance they'll offer a decent salary. Just go and give it a try."
Durai took out his mobile phone and started dialling a number.
Arjun hesitated for a moment.
What kind of job could he possibly get in a department meant for women? He wasn't particularly comfortable interacting with women either.
But at this point, he had no other choice.
Sathya's wedding was what mattered most.
"Okay, da. Ask him," Arjun said calmly.
Durai began speaking on the phone.
"Yes, sir... I have a friend named Arjun... He's really talented... What kind of job did you say? Oh, an assistant position? Okay, sir. I'll let him know."
After ending the call, Durai looked at Arjun.
"It's a big fashion design company, da. There's a lady designer named Mia. Apparently, she's looking for a personal assistant. But there's one condition... She wants only a female assistant."
Arjun was stunned.
"What? A female assistant? What kind of situation is this? How am I supposed to go there?"
"I know, da. Even I'm not sure what to do. Apparently, she's very strict too. She doesn't even allow men anywhere near her. But they say the salary is pretty good."
Arjun had no idea what to do. The job that was available wasn't open to him because he was a man.
He thought for a moment.
Sathya's wedding, the mounting debts and his fear about the future — everything flashed before his eyes.
"We have to do something and somehow get this job, da. Otherwise, I'll end up in serious trouble."
A determination began building inside him. No matter what the job was or how strange the situation might be, he had to find a way out of this debt.
Durai thought for a moment.
"I have an idea, da. Have you watched the movie Avvai Shanmugi?"
Arjun nodded.
"Hmm."
"Why don't you dress up as a woman, just like in that movie?" Durai suggested seriously.
"That's all possible in movies, da! How can I do that in real life? I'm not going there for just one or two days. I'll have to go every day. Won't they find out?"
"Before they find out, you can clear your debts and get out of there, right? Think about it, da. I know how bad your situation is. This is a chance. If you don't take risks, you won't get anything, machi!"
Durai tried to encourage him.
Arjun thought about it again. A sense of determination appeared in his eyes.
"Okay, da. I don't have any other option. I'm ready. But how? How am I going to become a woman? I don't know anything about this!"
Durai smiled.
"That's why I'm here! Don't worry. I'll help you. There's an old film makeup artist we know. With his help, we can change your appearance so much that nobody will recognise you. After that, I'll teach you everything — how to walk, how to dress and even how to speak like a woman. And make sure you watch Avvai Shanmugi, Varalaru and Remo at least once."
Arjun looked into Durai's eyes.
A shiver ran through him.
This was going to be a huge challenge. A new life. A completely new identity.
"Then what will my name be?" Arjun asked.
"Hmm..." Durai thought for a moment.
"Anjali! How does that sound? Anjali is a nice name. It gives the impression of a quiet and well-mannered girl."
Arjun slowly nodded.
"Anjali... Arjun... I think my entire life is about to change."
